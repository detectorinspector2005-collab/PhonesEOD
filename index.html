PH Labor Code
Art. 85 — 60-min meal period for shifts over 5 hrs. Total off-floor: 1.5 hrs. Afterhours 11:30 (or 10:30 during DST) always use 15-60-15 with B2 pinned to 3–4 PM (or 2–3 PM during DST).
